Privacy Policy — Carpet Cleaning Simulator

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Carpet Cleaning Simulator (the "Game") is made by Elena Malgina, an independent developer based in Thailand, publishing as Carpet Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

1. The short version

  • The Game has no ads and no tracking.
  • You do not create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server that receives them.
  • Purchases and the Pro subscription are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not use any analytics or advertising SDK.

2. Information stored on your device

  • What is stored: game progress (coins, unlocked tools and rugs, finished orders), settings (sound, graphics, language, interface size), tutorial status, and the App Store transaction IDs of coin purchases, kept only so that one purchase is never credited twice. They contain no name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • Where it stays: in the Game's private storage on your device. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup under Apple's terms.
  • Who can see it: we cannot.
  • How to delete it: use "Reset progress" in Settings, or delete the Game.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

  • In-app purchases (coin packs) and the Pro subscription are processed by Apple under the App Store terms.
  • Apple tells the Game only whether a purchase is valid, so that content can be unlocked. We do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

4. Statistics and crash reports from Apple

  • Apple may show us aggregated statistics about the Game (downloads, sessions, retention, purchases by country). These figures do not identify you.
  • Crash reports reach us only if you agreed to share them with app developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. They contain technical data about the crash and no personal information.

5. Network connections

The Game connects to the internet only for App Store purchases (through Apple) and when you open a link from the Game (this policy, support, terms) in your browser. The Game does not send your progress or any identifier anywhere.

6. Children

  • The Game is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed in the Kids category.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side; everything is on your device and under your control. If you believe this is not the case, or you have any question under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA or Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, contact us and we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Thailand this is the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

8. Security

  • The Game does not transmit your progress anywhere.
  • Communication with Apple uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the changes are significant, we will say so in the Game.
