Privacy Policy — Carpet Cleaning Simulator

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Carpet Cleaning Simulator (the "Game") is made by Elena Malgina (Carpet Cleaning Game), an individual developer based in Thailand ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game on iPhone, iPad or Mac, and the choices you have.

1. The short version

  • You do not create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server that receives them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • You can play without allowing tracking. Ads will then be less personalised.

2. Information stored on your device

  • What we store: game progress (coins, unlocked tools and rugs, finished orders), settings (sound, graphics, language), tutorial status, and the App Store transaction IDs of coin purchases, only so that one purchase is never credited twice (they contain no name, Apple ID or payment details).
  • Where it stays: in the Game's private storage on your device. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, under Apple's terms.
  • Who can see it: we cannot.
  • How to delete it: use "Reset progress" in Settings, or delete the Game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows full-screen ads between orders and optional rewarded ads. They are provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

The SDK may collect:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers, such as the Identifier for Advertising (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, or an app-scoped identifier;
  • advertising data and interactions with ads;
  • diagnostic and performance data (for example crash logs).

Google uses this to deliver, measure and personalise ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services. Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices:

  • Apple tracking (App Tracking Transparency). The Game asks once whether it may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before any ads are personalised, a consent form from Google (User Messaging Platform) asks for your consent. You can change your choice any time in the Game: Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Pro subscribers do not see full-screen ads.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

  • In-app purchases and the Pro subscription are processed by Apple under the App Store terms.
  • Apple tells us only whether a purchase is valid, so we can unlock content. We do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

5. Analytics and crash reports

  • We do not add our own analytics SDK.
  • Apple may share aggregated statistics with us (downloads, sessions, crashes), and crash reports only if you agreed to share them with app developers in iOS settings. These do not identify you to us.

6. Children

  • The Game is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game in a way that involved personal data, contact us and we will help.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Personalised advertising: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised advertising and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game.
  • Purchases: performance of the contract with you, handled by Apple.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA, or Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562), you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete your personal data;
  • object to or restrict processing;
  • withdraw consent;
  • data portability;
  • opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

How to use these rights:

  • Data held by Google: use the in-game "Ad privacy" option and iOS tracking settings, or the tools at https://myaccount.google.com.
  • Data on your device: you control it directly.
  • Anything else: contact us using the details below. We will reply within 30 days.

You may also complain to your data protection authority. In Thailand, this is the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

9. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, using safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Retention

  • Data on your device: kept until you reset progress or delete the Game.
  • Data processed by Google: retained according to Google's policies.

11. Security

  • The Game does not transmit your progress anywhere.
  • Communication with Apple and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. Changes

  • If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
  • If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the Game.
